    How to read the course

    Derby listens to the Pons launch factory on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). Every new token enters the field as a horse at the starting gate. How far down the track it stands is how much ETH sits in its bonding curve; the distance posts are marked in ETH, and the winning post is 4.2 ETH, where the token graduates to a DEX. The scale is stretched so the early furlongs, where most of the racing happens, are readable.

    Buys kick up dust and push a horse forward, sells pull it back. A horse that goes fifteen minutes without a trade slows to a walk, and two minutes later it is pulled up. Every horse carries the silks of its jockey, the wallet that launched it. Most jockeys ride once, so the form comes from the stable: every wallet funded by the same trainer, and how their earlier launches ran.

    Stable
    The jockey is the wallet that launched the token; most are brand new. The trainer is the wallet that first sent the jockey ETH, and the stable is every wallet that trainer funded. A trainer with more than 5,000 transactions is treated as an exchange or a bridge and not counted.

    The stable's earlier launches, newest on the right. 1 past the post · 2 reached 3 ETH · 3 reached 2 ETH · 4 reached 1 ETH · 0 traded, under 1 ETH · P under 0.1 ETH volume · U unseated: the jockey sold most of what they bought.

    How to read the track

    Derby turns Pons launches on Robinhood Chain into a horse race. Ten seconds and you're in.

    1. A horse is a new tokenEvery token launched on Pons in the last two hours runs here.
    2. Distance is ETH in its curveBuys push it forward, sells pull it back. The finish is 4.2 ETH: the token graduates to a DEX.
    3. Odds show where the buys go3/1 means it took about a quarter of all buys in the last 10 minutes. EVS or 2/1 is a favourite, 50/1 an outsider. A picture of the crowd, not a prediction.
    4. On fire: the dev burned tokensThe launcher sent part of their own bag to the dead address. Those tokens are gone for good.
    5. Yellow flag: a check spotted somethingThe dev sold, a crowd of snipers at launch, a copied name. A pattern worth a look, not proof.
    6. Pick three: a free gamePick up to three runners in the current race. Points every time they pass 1, 2, 3 ETH and the finish. Long odds pay more. No money involved.
